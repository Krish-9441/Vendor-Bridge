Platform

Six modules. One procurement lifecycle.

Each screen is built around a real handoff in the process — not a generic settings page.

01

Vendor Management

Registration, GST and contact details, categories, and status tracking — with search and filtering to keep a large vendor base usable.

02

RFQ Creation

Titles, product or service specs, quantities, attachments, deadlines and vendor assignment — issued in one structured form.

03

Quotation Comparison

Side-by-side pricing, delivery timelines and vendor ratings, with the lowest price and fastest delivery surfaced automatically.

04

Approval Workflow

Approve or reject with remarks, see the full approval timeline, and track state transitions from submitted to signed off.

05

Purchase Orders & Invoicing

Auto-generated PO numbers, tax and total calculations, and invoices that can be downloaded, printed, or emailed directly.

06

Reports & Analytics

Vendor performance, spending summaries and monthly procurement trends, exportable for finance and leadership review.

Workflow

How a single request moves end to end

This is the actual trail a request leaves in VendorBridge, from first RFQ to a paid invoice.

01

Officer creates an RFQ

Specs, quantities and a deadline are set, and vendors are invited to quote.

02

Vendors submit quotations

Pricing, delivery timelines and notes come back into the same RFQ record.

03

Quotations are compared

The procurement team reviews all responses side by side before deciding.

04

Approval is requested

The selected quotation is routed to a manager for sign-off, with remarks.

05

Purchase order is generated

An approved quotation becomes a numbered PO, ready to send to the vendor.

06

Invoice is generated

Taxes and totals are calculated from the PO, with no re-entry of line items.

07

Invoice is printed or emailed

Sent directly from the record — as a PDF, by print, or straight to the vendor's inbox.

08

Activity is logged

Every step above is timestamped in the activity log, ready for audit or reporting.
